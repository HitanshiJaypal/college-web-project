Our Campus Facilities

Library

A huge collection of textbooks, journals, and reference books for all courses, providing a conducive environment for research and learning.

Computer Labs

Fully equipped labs with latest software, high-speed internet, and smart boards to support practical learning and projects.

Electronics & Robotics Labs

Our Electronics & Robotics Labs provide students with hands-on experience using the latest equipment in electronics, automation, and robotics. These labs support practical learning, research projects, and innovation, preparing students for industry and advanced studies.

Sports Facilities

Football, Basketball, Cricket, Gymnasium, and Yoga halls for all-round fitness and extracurricular development.

Auditorium & Seminar Halls

Modern halls for workshops, conferences, cultural events, and guest lectures.

Cafeteria & Common Areas

Relaxation and social spaces for students with healthy food options and comfortable seating areas.
